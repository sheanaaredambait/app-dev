# My Favorite Series: Loki

**Loki** is a sci-fi superhero series set in the *Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)* that follows the God of Mischief after escaping with the Tesseract.

## Quick Overview
* **Genre:** Sci-Fi / Action / Multiverse
* **Main Character:** Loki (played by Tom Hiddleston)
* **Key Faction:** Time Variance Authority (TVA)

## Why I Like It
1. **Multiverse Concepts:** It explores timeline branches, nexus events, and variants.
2. **Character Growth:** Loki transitions from a villain into a true protector of time.
3. **Visuals & Score:** The retro-futuristic TVA aesthetics and synthesizer soundtrack are unmatched.

> *"I know what kind of god I need to be. For you. For all of us."* — Loki
